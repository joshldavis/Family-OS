# FOS-007 household erasure gate

The School Agent ledger stores a child's assignment title, student identifier,
due date, the approving parent's Auth ID, source metadata, and action history.
The FOS-006 proposal makes approved proposals and executed claims immutable,
restricts deletion of review evidence, and uses `ON DELETE RESTRICT` for
referenced Auth users and students. Those choices protect a review from being
rewritten, but **ordinary account or household deletion cannot complete**
once an action has been executed. A household cascade is not an erasure plan.

The disposable PostgreSQL rehearsal in
`tests/database/fos007_erasure_blockers.sql` proves that an executed proposal,
executed claim, and referenced Auth user cannot be deleted by ordinary SQL.
It also confirms the service role lacks receipt deletion privileges. The
rehearsal rolls back and never targets a shared or production database.

Before activation, define a privileged deletion workflow that:

1. Revokes the household's School Agent sources and active sessions, then
   blocks new School Agent writes while deletion is in progress.
2. Identifies every school source, proposal, consent, review, claim,
   execution receipt, and generated assignment by the household UUID.
   Verify that an assignment was generated by the agent before deleting it;
   user-created assignments need their own deletion policy.
3. Deletes dependents in a reviewed order inside one transaction, with a
   dedicated erasure authority. The normal service role must not gain blanket
   receipt deletion or trigger-bypass privileges. Confirm no rows remain for
   that household and no other household changed.
4. Handles external school message copies, uploads, logs, backups, and Auth
   account deletion separately. Database deletion alone does not erase them.

Product and privacy owners still need to choose retention periods, whether
completed review evidence may be erased immediately on request, how shared
caregivers and children spanning households are handled, and what minimal
nonpersonal operational audit remains. Until those decisions are made and a
full isolated erasure rehearsal passes, **do not activate the execution route
or run the FOS-006/FOS-007 proposals as migrations**.
